About Us

A Decade of Garden Excellence

Since 2013, Three Leaves Gardens has been caring for some of Auckland's finest residential gardens. We bring a meticulous eye, deep horticultural knowledge, and genuine passion to every property we work on.

What sets us apart is our attention to detail and our specialisation in hedge work — precision trimming and shaping that transforms boundaries into living architecture. Whether it's a one-off garden refresh or ongoing maintenance, we treat your garden as if it were our own.
