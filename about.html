About Three Leaves Gardens

Twelve years of caring for Auckland's finest gardens.

Est. 2013

From a Passion to a Profession

Three Leaves Gardens began in 2013 with a simple belief: that a garden deserves the same care and craftsmanship as any other part of a beautiful home. What started as a one-person operation has grown into a trusted team serving discerning homeowners across Auckland.

Over the years, we've developed a particular reputation for hedge work — the precision trimming and shaping that turns a boundary into a feature. It's a craft that demands patience, a good eye, and the right tools, and it's one we've honed on hundreds of hedges across the city.

We work on gardens of all sizes, but our heart is in high-end residential properties where quality matters and details are noticed. Whether it's a grand formal garden or a compact urban retreat, we bring the same standard of care.
